# Content and browser checklist

## Replace the starter content

- Decide whether the site is primarily a profile, portfolio, notebook, or studio.
- Replace Field Notes, The Lab, and Things Kept with real destinations.
- Add a title, short description, status, and URL for each destination.
- Replace the introductory biography with final copy.
- Confirm the preferred contact method and social profiles.

## Browser and device review

- Check the current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge releases.
- Check one iPhone, one Android phone, and a tablet-sized viewport.
- Test the navigation, theme switch, anchor links, and clock around a minute change.
- Test keyboard navigation and visible focus from the top of the page to the footer.
- Check light and dark themes with reduced motion enabled.
- Run Lighthouse for accessibility, performance, SEO, and best practices.
- Confirm that the social preview image renders after the metadata PR is merged.

## Release decision

- Review all copy and outbound links.
- Merge the accessibility and metadata PRs after visual review.
- Verify `www.naik.dev` and `naik.dev` after the final deployment.
